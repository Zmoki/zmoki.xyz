## Where I am

I'm still in Tbilisi, Georgia. The beginning of 2026 was nervous. A new labor migration law was going to require all foreigners working in Georgia, including individual entrepreneurs like me, to get a work permit and a residence permit by May 1. I was worried I wouldn't manage it all and would have to urgently move to Armenia. In April new amendments arrived, and individual entrepreneurs working with foreign clients no longer need the permits. I breathed a sigh of relief and stayed.

I still spend most of my time at home, and I've started going out and meeting people more often. I joined an English language group for artists, mostly online with some in-person meetings. Through it I learned about the Caucasian Art Circle. I also gave a presentation to a local business community about [my marketing work](/feed/18-technical-half/) over the past year and a half. It was my first presentation since 2021.

In May I went to Istanbul and reunited with my friend after four years apart. I love Istanbul, and those were an amazing few days.

I recently celebrated my 39th birthday. Even though I now have dozens of friends in Tbilisi I could invite to a party, I decided to spend the day alone at a new spa hotel. It was amazing.

## Art and creative work

I originally planned my exhibition for March 2026, then decided I didn't want to rush it and gave myself more time. I keep making collages and experimenting with color and shape. I designed a way to store and transport them safely, which is a real task given their depth and fragility.

The English lessons for artists helped a lot. My English is decent for a non-native speaker, and most of it is technical vocabulary. Translating it into the art world was an interesting challenge. Now I read curatorial texts differently. I see where they are complete bullshit, and I understand how to write a good art text.

Right now Tbilisi is very hot and I have no desire to make collages. I bought a large stock of paints and application tools, and I'm waiting for the heat to break so I can try them all.

## This digital garden

I created illustrations for [my posts](/feed/2-why-im-building-a-digital-garden/) that remind me a bit of my collages. I'm very happy with how the website looks now. I'm finishing a round of design and [SEO improvements](/feed/13-new-website-seo-checklist/), and after that I'll get back to writing. I have many new posts and even more ideas to share. I also realized I can turn my posts into content for LinkedIn and Instagram.

## Tech work

My first client decided to leave Webflow, and I migrated their site to the Astro framework. I've migrated sites from legacy stacks before. The previous project, in 2021, took a year and a team of eight people. This time, with AI, I did it alone in a quarter. And I've enabled the marketers to work with the site directly using AI.

I have new clients too. One of them will be migrating their site from WordPress to Astro. It's a very interesting process.

For the first time since becoming an independent consultant, I've reached the income I used to earn as a full-time employee. I'm very proud of myself.

## GoodINP

I analyzed my work over the past year and a half and noticed a problem clients kept bringing me. It grew into [a product idea, GoodINP](/feed/17-site-speed-work-that-became-goodinp/). INP, or interaction to next paint, is Google's metric for how fast a site responds when people interact with it, and GoodINP helps businesses fix it. I've thought the product through, and I'm not building it yet. In this attempt at a startup I decided to start with sales rather than development. B2B sales are now my main focus outside client and art work.

## Learning and growth

I haven't had anxiety for a year. Learning about [my neurodivergence](/feed/4-neurodivergent-glossary/), joining a support group, and [adapting my life and work](/feed/5-day-themes-system/) to it helped enormously. Now I'm focused on the physiological side of neurodivergence. I'm looking into joint hypermobility, POTS (a condition where your heart rate jumps when you stand up), and MCAS (overactive mast cells that cause allergy-like reactions). I'm adapting my diet and lifestyle, and I feel better physically, not only psychologically.

## Entertainment and rest

I'm fascinated by the Stargate universe. I watched all the canonical films and series, SG-1, Atlantis, Universe, Continuum, The Ark of Truth. For a long time I couldn't figure out what it's really about. Propaganda for the US military machine? The power of the human mind? Finally I got it. Stargate is about the nature of God. I'm not a religious person and a proponent of science, and it was interesting to see how religiosity and divinity can be viewed from a scientific perspective.
