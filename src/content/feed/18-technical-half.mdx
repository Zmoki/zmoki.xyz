## Where I go deep

The AI part is where I'm strongest. Real AI work, from getting found by AI search to building the systems underneath it.

- **Get found by AI search.** I make sites that AI assistants quote and cite, so you show up where buyers now look.
- **Relevance engineering.** I match your content to what people actually search and ask, using embeddings, the numeric fingerprints AI models use to compare meaning.
- **Build the AI itself.** Embeddings analysis, question matching, retrieval. I build these systems myself.
- **Wire AI into the work.** I plug AI tools into your marketing stack so the team ships faster than before.
