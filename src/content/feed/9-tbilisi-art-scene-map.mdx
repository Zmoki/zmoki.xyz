I'm an immigrant. I [left Russia in 2022](/feed/1-about-me/#places-that-shaped-me), lived in Limassol, Cyprus, and now I live in Tbilisi, Georgia. Every move restarts my life from scratch, and art is how I put down roots in a new place.

In Cyprus I showed my work twice. My debut solo exhibition "Do you love the color of the sky?" grew out of [my daily photos of the dusty Cypriot sky](/feed/12-air-quality-awareness/), and my installation "The Blue" was part of the group exhibition "RE:SOURCE" in Limassol.

## I want to exhibit in Tbilisi

Now I make small paper collages. I've made over 40 of them, and I want to show them here, in Tbilisi, while I live here.

I once set myself a symbolic deadline for this exhibition. The deadline passed, and I was honest with myself, I'm not ready yet. I'll run the exhibition when I find the right gallery and feel ready, and I'm fine with not knowing the date.

I also [collect contemporary art](/feed/1-about-me/#my-art-and-collecting), buying pieces directly from living artists since 2021, and I would love to find works by Georgian artists for my collection too.

For both exhibiting and collecting, I need to know the local art scene.

## Building my exploration tool

Here's the challenge. I've lived in Tbilisi for two years, and I still haven't explored its art scene properly. My default is to stay home, working on my art and [tech projects](/feed/18-technical-half/).

To push myself out, I revived a project I had abandoned. **"Where's Art"** is a Notion database where I catalogue interesting art places around the world. It was dusty and incomplete. I blew off the dust and added every Tbilisi-based art institution, gallery, and artist-run space I knew of or could find.

I am still working on the Notion database, but I've already turned it into a public Google Map list.

### My Tbilisi art map

I'm sharing the map with you because I think it's a useful resource for anyone interested in the Tbilisi art scene.

<ResourceLink slug="google-maps-list-tbilisi-art-spaces" />

The list has **over 20 places**, a mix of art galleries, museums, and cultural centers. These are all actual, working spaces that I've confirmed hold exhibitions, maybe not every single day, but at least a few times per year.

## The next step

My plan is to turn this digital map into a real-world journey, one pin at a time. I want to visit these places, meet the people, and find a home for my story and my collages. And maybe a small work by a local artist will join my collection along the way.
